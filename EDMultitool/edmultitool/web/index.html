    Jump range, ly

    now — · full tank — · max —

    How far your ship jumps, worked out from its modules and engineering the way the game does.

    • now: with the fuel and cargo aboard at this moment.
    • full tank: tanks full, with the cargo you carry now.
    • max: an empty hold and only the fuel for one jump, the farthest single jump the ship can make.

    now and full tank count what you carry; max is the same best case as the game's own figure. Routes are planned with full tank (your routing range) unless you pick another in EDMC's settings.

        Ranks

          Percent to the next rank, as at your last login or promotion.

          Point at a rank for its whole ladder: every rank, what moves you up, what each rank opens, and yours marked.

          The emblems grow with the rank: wings, crests, chevrons, a wreath at Elite and a star for each Elite I–V.

          Superpower reputation

            How each superpower sees you, from −100 to +100, as at your last login.

            • Hostile below −90
            • Unfriendly from −90
            • Neutral from −35
            • Cordial from +4
            • Friendly from +35
            • Allied from +90

            Not the same as the navy rank: reputation rises with missions for that superpower's factions.

            Powerplay

              The power you are pledged to, your merits and your rank.

              • Rank: the game's own, 0 until you have done your power's introductory missions. Merits to the next rank follow the community's published table.
              • A Powerplay week begins on Thursday at 07:00 UTC.
              • Below: how many systems each power holds (Spansh), yours highlighted.

              Over time

              Your record over time, from your journals.

              • Superpower reputation and navy ranks as the game wrote them at each login or promotion: one point per day, the last of the day.
              • Powerplay merits you earned each week.

              Point at a chart for its values.

              Minor factions

              Faction Standing Missions State Last met Nearest

              Every minor faction you have a standing with, best first.

              The game writes your standing to its journal only when you arrive in a system where the faction is present, or log in there. Selling data, handing in a mission or anything else that changes it writes no number (a mission shows only "+" signs). So a station can already show you Allied while this card still shows the value from your last arrival: it catches up the next time you arrive or log in where the faction is.

              • Standing: the game's word and number, −100 to +100, with the same steps as the superpowers. Better standing brings more missions that pay more; a faction that holds you Hostile refuses you at its stations and its ships may attack. The EDMC panel warns you when you arrive where one is.
              • State: war, boom, election… what the faction was going through when you last met it.
              • Nearest: from the systems you have seen it in yourself, not from any online source. Copy the name for the galaxy map.

              Factions you met but have done nothing for (standing exactly 0) are only counted.

              Lifetime totals

                The game's own statistics, as it wrote them at your last login (it writes them only then): the same numbers Inara and other sites show, read from your journals. Nothing is asked online.

                Each line shows:

                • the total now;
                • what it grew by since the newest login at least a week earlier;
                • a small chart of the total at every login (point at it for the value that day).

                Earned and spent, all time

                Every credit the game has counted since the commander was created, by activity.

                • Earned: selling exploration and organic data, trade and mining, bounties, bonds…
                • Spent: outfitting, ships, rebuys, repairs and the rest.
                • Share: the part of the bar's total.

                The game does not split out missions.

                Deaths

                  The game's statistics count no deaths (only what rebuys cost in total), so each death is read from your journals one by one, newest first.

                  • Who killed you: a pilot with their ship and combat rank, every member of a wing, or a station's or settlement's defences. A death the game names no killer for (a crash, a self-destruct…) shows as such.
                  • What coming back cost: the rebuy, free, or bankrupt.

                  The game's lifetime totals for this activity at your last login; figures still at 0 are left out.

                  • +: grown since the newest login at least a week earlier.
                  • Lines in italics are worked out from the game's totals (a sum, an average, a rate), not written by the game; mission rewards are not in them.

                  Earned and spent

                  CrThis sessionToday This week

                  What you earned and spent, from your journals.

                  • Trade profit: what you sold for minus what you paid (mined or found cargo counts in full). Bought cargo is not spending until it is sold.
                  • This session runs from the game's start; today from midnight; this week from Monday, both by this computer's clock.

                  Balance over time

                  Your credits over time: exact at every login, worked out between logins from every credit earned, spent or paid for cargo. The live balance ends the line.

                  Point at the line for the balance on that day.

                  Still carried, not sold

                  Lost if you die before you sell it.

                    What you carry and have not sold yet.

                    • Cartographic data: every star and planet you scanned since you last sold that system, valued with the first-discovery, first-mapped and probe bonuses your scans show. An estimate.
                    • Exobiology: species analysed since your last sale at Vista Genomics, at their base value (a first-logged bonus is not counted).

                    About ED Multitool

                    A companion for Elite Dangerous that runs inside EDMC: where you are and how far your ship jumps; routes with the best scan and exobiology stops; the nearest market, engineer, service or Powerplay system; trade and cargo sales; missions, earnings and your commander's record.
